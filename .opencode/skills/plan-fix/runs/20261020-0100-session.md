## Decisions

Approach: Option A - the department page resolves its faculty through the SAME chain the top-level
page uses, so both surfaces cover the same faculty and print the same pooled numbers.

WHAT CHANGES
- lib/evaluation-utils.ts: NEW resolveDepartmentFaculties(departmentId, candidateFacultyIds, ctx)
  - the pure INVERSE of the existing resolveFacultyDepartment. One rule, used forwards by the
  top-level page and backwards by the department page, so the two can never drift apart.
- features/evaluation-results/evaluation-results.repository.ts: the chain-context loader (period ->
  semesterId, users -> departmentId, faculty_subjects -> sections -> department_courses, ~60 lines)
  is EXTRACTED into one private function shared by listLiveDeptAverages and the new
  listDepartmentFacultyIds. Copying it would recreate the three-variant problem.
  NEW listDepartmentFacultyIds(departmentId, evaluationPeriodId): candidates = the period's
  submitted evaluatee ids (same filter as listLiveDeptAverages: status SUBMITTED, isDisabled false,
  facultySubjectId not null); context = loadDeptResolutionContext; result =
  resolveDepartmentFaculties(...).
- lib/types/evaluation.ts: IEvaluationResultRepository += listDepartmentFacultyIds.
- features/evaluation-results/evaluation-results.service.ts: facIds come from
  evaluationResultRepository.listDepartmentFacultyIds(departmentId, evaluationPeriodId) instead of
  userRepository.listByDepartment(departmentId); the names come from
  userRepository.listByIds(facIds, { includeDeleted: true }) - the chain does not filter deletedAt,
  so a soft-deleted faculty is IN the set and its row would read "Unknown" without this.

INVARIANTS PRESERVED
- API routes stay thin: the membership resolution lives in the repository (it owns the chain
  context) plus a pure helper - not in the route or the page. The route and the service's
  response shape are unchanged.
- All Supabase access via repositories/DI: the new method is on the repository; the service calls
  it through the factory. No direct supabase in the service or the page.
- proxy.ts access enforcement: untouched. Role pipe-delimited priority: n/a.
- Double-click via SubmitButton: n/a, read-only view.

CONSEQUENCE
A faculty with no users.departmentId but assigned to a section of the department is INVISIBLE on
the department results page (admin and dean) but IS counted by the top-level page and by the
cached rows. The department page therefore under-reports the department's faculty, its response
count and its pooled average.

Teach (6=A): why it behaves this way - the department page was written against the simplest
question ("who is in this department?") and the simplest answer (users.departmentId). The chain
was added later, for faculty who are assigned to a section but have no departmentId on their user
row - the admin data (users.departmentId) and the teaching data (faculty_subjects -> sections ->
department_courses) can disagree, and the chain prefers the user row and falls back to the
teaching data. The top-level page and the compute path were migrated to the chain; the department
page was not, so it is the last surface still answering the question the old way. Why it matters
now: the two pages are supposed to be the same number, and they are not, for exactly the faculty a
dean would most expect to see. When it bites: only for departments that contain faculty without a
users.departmentId - for every other department the two sets are identical and nothing changes.

## Outcome

All 4 slices applied. The department page and the top-level page now resolve membership the SAME
way, from the same candidate population, with the same chain:

  users.departmentId  --else-->  faculty_subjects(semesterId) --> sections --> department_courses
        (both surfaces, one loader: loadDeptResolutionContext)

The forward direction (which department is this faculty in?) and the backward direction (which
faculty are in this department?) are now the SAME pure helper - resolveFacultyDepartment and its
new inverse resolveDepartmentFaculties - so they cannot disagree.

The chain context is loaded by ONE module-private function, shared by listLiveDeptAverages and the
new listDepartmentFacultyIds; the staged optimisation (the faculty_subjects/sections/
department_courses lookups run only for the faculty WITHOUT a users.departmentId) survives, and
listLiveDeptAverages' behaviour is unchanged - its 8 tests plus the top-level and export route
tests are the guard.

Effect: a faculty with no users.departmentId but assigned to one of the department's sections now
appears on the department results page (admin and dean) - they were invisible there before - and
the two surfaces print the same pooled numbers. A soft-deleted faculty is also in the set (the
chain does not filter deletedAt), so the service fetches their row with includeDeleted: true
rather than printing "Unknown".

KNOWN RESIDUAL (documented, out of scope): the stale-eval window - the department page hard-filters
evals whose evaluateeId != faculty_subject.faculty_id, while the top level only excludes them
once computeAll has marked them isDisabled. The window self-closes after the next recompute.
Also unchanged: getDeanDetails still resolves by users.departmentId only.
